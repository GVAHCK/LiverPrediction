# TRIPOD+AI Reporting Checklist for Machine Learning Prediction Models

This document satisfies the reporting requirements of the **TRIPOD+AI statement** (*Transparent Reporting of a Multivariable Prediction Model for Individual Prognosis or Diagnosis — Artificial Intelligence*, BMJ 2024;385:e078378).

| Item # | Item Description | Location in Manuscript / Codebase | Adherence & Reporting Details |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|
| **Title** | Identify the study as developing, evaluating, or auditing a prediction model, specifying AI/ML approach and target condition. | Title (`paper.tex:L1`) | "Referral-Budgeted, Sex-Audited Selective Classification for Small-Sample Liver Laboratory Data." Explicitly names target and methodology. |
| **Abstract** | Structured summary of background, objectives, data sources, eligibility criteria, sample size, outcome, modeling, performance, fairness, and interpretation. | Abstract (`paper.tex:Section Abstract`) | Fully structured into Background, Methods, Results, Limitations, and Conclusion. Reports exact numbers (AUROC 0.7472, specificity 23.0%, Brier 0.1740). |
| **Background** | Clinical and technological rationale for developing or auditing the algorithm. | Introduction (`paper.tex:Section 1`) | Motivates failure modes of standard small-sample ML: winner's curse, leaky CV thresholds, severe female false-negative rates (FNR), and unbudgeted selective triage. |
| **Objectives** | Explicit statement of hypotheses and secondary exploratory comparisons. | Introduction (`paper.tex:Section 1.2`) & `reports/PREREG.md` | Pre-registered hypotheses H1–H3; primary comparison at referral budget $B=0.20$ formally registered prior to execution. |
| **Source of Data** | Describe study design, cohort origin, sampling dates, and ethical framework. | Data Cohorts (`paper.tex:Section 2.1`) | Retrospective Indian Liver Patient Dataset (ILPD, Andhra Pradesh, $N=583$) and secondary UCI HCV replication ($N=615$, Göttingen cohort, CC BY 4.0). |
| **Participants** | Eligibility criteria, recruitment setting, and demographic characteristics. | Data Cohorts (`paper.tex:Table 1`) | Full demographic table reporting median age, sex distribution (441 M / 142 F in ILPD), and baseline biomarker distributions with IQRs. |
| **Outcome** | Definition and ascertainment method of the target clinical outcome. | Data Cohorts (`paper.tex:Section 2.2`) | Binary retrospective liver disease diagnosis (ILPD: Class 1 = patient with liver disease, recoded from raw $2 \to 0$ non-disease). |
| **Predictors** | Precise definitions of all clinical input features, laboratory units, and missingness. | Data Cohorts (`paper.tex:Section 2.3`) | 10 laboratory features: Age, Total Bilirubin, Direct Bilirubin, ALP, ALT, AST, Total Proteins, Albumin, A/G Ratio, and recorded biological Sex. |
| **Sample Size** | Sample size justification and effective sample size across demographic strata. | Data Cohorts (`paper.tex:Section 2.4`) | Complete cohort ($N=583$, 416 disease, 167 controls). Transparently highlights female subgroup sparsity ($n=142$). |
| **Missing Data** | Handling of missing data and audit of potential non-random mechanisms. | Robustness (`paper.tex:Section 4.3`) | 4 missing values in A/G ratio handled via median imputation inside pipelines; evaluated under 10%–30% synthetic MCAR and marker-dependent stress tests. |
| **Nested CV Protocol** | Explicit protocol preventing selection leakage across hyperparameters, models, and thresholds. | Methodology (`paper.tex:Section 3.1`) | 5-fold $\times$ 3 repeats outer CV with inner 3-fold CV. 48-combination grid search evaluated exclusively on inner out-of-fold predictions. |
| **Selective Policy** | Formulation of referral-budgeted, subgroup-audited selective classification rule. | Methodology (`paper.tex:Section 3.2`) | 3-way triage per sex: $(a_g, b_g)$ minimizing $\max_g(\text{MissedRate}_g) + \lambda|\Delta r|$ subject to overall referral $\le B$ and coverage $\ge 50\%$. |
| **Evaluation Metrics** | Discrimination, calibration, clinical utility, and selective coverage. | Experiments (`paper.tex:Section 3.4`) | AUROC, Average Precision (AP), screening recall, binary specificity, Brier score, calibration slope/intercept, selective risk, worst-group miss rate, and referral gap. |
| **Statistical Analysis** | Avoidance of fold-level statistical tests; valid patient-level inference. | Statistical Rigor (`paper.tex:Section 3.5`) | Paired patient-level repeat-aware bootstrap (2,000 resamples); Holm-Bonferroni family-wise error rate control for secondary baselines. Replaced L03 fold-level DeLong test. |
| **Results: Discrimination** | Performance of outer holdout predictions. | Results (`paper.tex:Section 4.1`, Table 2) | Outer AUROC 0.7472 [0.7382, 0.7562], AP 0.8909 [0.8830, 0.8989]. |
| **Results: Trade-offs** | Plain and honest disclosure of clinical operating trade-offs. | Results (`paper.tex:Section 4.1`, Table 2) | Emphasizes that high screening sensitivity (91.2%) forces low binary specificity (23.0%), necessitating an abstention/referral mechanism. |
| **Results: Selective Triage** | Performance across referral budgets $B \in \{0.10, 0.20, 0.30\}$. | Results (`paper.tex:Section 4.2`, Table 3) | Worst-group missed disease rate reduced from 15.5% (no abstention) to 5.0% ($B=0.30$). Reports female sample size constraint infeasibility at $B=0.10$. |
| **Results: Hypothesis Testing** | Confirmatory testing outcome. | Statistical Results (`paper.tex:Section 4.2`, Table 4) | Confirmatory primary test at $B=0.20$: $\Delta = -0.47\%$ (95% CI: $[-4.88\%, +3.33\%], p=0.8680$). Fails to reject $H_0$ at small sample size; reported honestly without data spinning. |
| **Interpretability** | Global feature contributions and failure mode profiling. | Interpretability (`paper.tex:Section 4.4`, Fig 4, Fig 5) | Standardized odds ratios (ALT OR 1.69, DB OR 1.56, Albumin OR 0.66). False-negative profiling reveals deceptive normal enzymes in female liver patients. |
| **Limitations** | Discussion of sample size, geographic concentration, and clinical gaps. | Discussion (`paper.tex:Section 5`) | Retrospective cross-sectional design, single-region cohort, lack of clinical costs, binary sex categorization, calibration $\neq$ conformal guarantee. |
| **Reproducibility** | Full availability of code, deterministic seeds, environment locks, and artifacts. | Data & Code Statement (`paper.tex:Section 6`) | Deterministic seed 42, lockfiles, one-command Makefile rebuild, verification script (`src/verify_paper_numbers.py`). |
